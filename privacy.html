Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Gridlock Holmes. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit or use the website at gridlock-holmes.com (the "Site"), including its CSS Grid learning resources, interactive tutorials, visual grid builder and Grid Detective tool.

For privacy-related enquiries, contact us at jon.ellisdon@gridlock-holmes.com.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

If you contact us by email, report a bug, request support or suggest a feature, you may provide information such as your name or chosen username, email address, the contents of your message or enquiry, and any other information you choose to include.

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, provide requested support and consider your feedback. The Site does not currently offer a newsletter or email subscription.

2.2 Information stored in your browser

Gridlock Holmes uses browser storage technologies, including localStorage, to support website features. Depending on how you use the Site, information stored in your browser may include:

  • Saved CSS Grid layouts and configurations
  • Interface preferences
  • Learning progress and completed lessons
  • Optional student usernames or identifiers
  • Student login credentials, including passwords
  • Game progress and achievements
  • Cookie-consent preferences

This information is stored locally in your browser rather than maintained as a central user account on our servers. We do not provide a server-side student account service. People who use the same browser or device may be able to access information stored there, including student login credentials. The security of locally stored credentials depends on the security of your device and browser. Never reuse a password from another account for a student login on the Site.

Clearing your browser's website data or local storage may permanently delete saved layouts, preferences, credentials, learning progress and game progress. We cannot recover locally stored information if it is deleted or lost.

If you choose to share a layout or generated configuration through a shareable link, information included in that link may be accessible to anyone who has the link. Do not enter confidential or sensitive personal information into browser-based tools or optional student profiles.

2.3 Automatically collected technical information

When you visit or use the Site, technical and usage information may be collected automatically. This may include:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Pages visited and features used
  • Time and date of visits
  • Referring website or traffic source
  • Website usage and interaction information
  • Technical information relating to errors, security and website performance

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Site and its features
  • Respond to enquiries, support requests, bug reports and feedback
  • Understand how visitors use the Site and improve its content, tools and performance
  • Monitor website performance, prevent abuse and protect the Site
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Support website advertising if advertising services are introduced in the future

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where UK data protection law applies, we process personal information using an appropriate lawful basis. Depending on the information and the purpose, this may include:

  • Legitimate interests: for example, operating, maintaining and securing the Site, and responding to enquiries. We consider the impact on your rights and interests when relying on this basis.
  • Consent: where required for non-essential technologies or optional communications. The Site does not currently offer a newsletter or email subscription.
  • Legal obligation: where processing is necessary to comply with a legal requirement.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal will not affect processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

5. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors interact with Gridlock Holmes. Depending on the configuration of our analytics services and your consent preferences, Google Analytics may process information such as pages visited, interactions with website features, browser and device information, approximate geographic information, referring sources and online identifiers. We use this information to understand Site usage and improve our content and tools.

For information about Google's privacy practices, see Google's Privacy Policy. You can also opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

For details about cookies and consent choices on the Site, see our Cookie Policy.

6. Google AdSense and Future Advertising

As of the publication date of this Policy, Gridlock Holmes is applying to participate in Google AdSense. Google AdSense advertising has not yet been implemented on the Site, and we are not currently displaying Google AdSense advertisements.

If our application is approved and advertising is introduced, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses and other identifiers when advertisements are served. This information may be used to deliver and display advertisements, measure advertising performance, limit advertisement frequency, detect and prevent invalid advertising activity, and provide personalised or non-personalised advertising, depending on available settings, applicable law and your consent choices.

Third-party advertising providers may place or read cookies on your browser, or use similar technologies, when advertisements are displayed. We will update this Policy when advertising is introduced so that it accurately describes the services and technologies being used.

You can review Google advertising settings and Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Sharing Information with Service Providers

We may use third-party providers to operate and improve the Site. Depending on the services enabled, these may include:

  • Netlify: website hosting, delivery and technical infrastructure
  • Google Analytics: website usage measurement and analytics
  • Google AdSense and advertising partners: only if advertising is implemented in the future

The Site also uses CookieYes to display and manage cookie-consent preferences. Further information about cookies and consent is available in our Cookie Policy.

These providers may process information on our behalf or in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable contractual or legal requirements. We may also disclose information where necessary to comply with the law, respond to lawful requests, or protect the rights, security and operation of the Site.

The Site's contact page uses email links. We do not use FormSubmit to process contact forms, and we do not currently use a newsletter or email subscription provider.

8. International Data Transfers

Some third-party providers used to operate the Site may process information in countries outside the United Kingdom. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will use appropriate legal safeguards where required by applicable data-protection legislation.

9. Children's Privacy

Gridlock Holmes provides educational resources that may be used by students, educators and learners of different ages. We encourage parents, guardians and educators to supervise younger users and to help them avoid entering identifiable personal information into optional student profiles or browser-based tools.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 through server-side student accounts. The Site does not operate server-side student accounts. However, a child may choose to provide information through an email enquiry or enter information into a browser-based feature. Parents, guardians and educators should supervise younger users accordingly.

Where any part of the Site is directed towards children, we will take account of applicable children's privacy requirements and advertising restrictions.

10. Data Security

We use appropriate measures intended to protect information handled through the Site, and we use HTTPS encryption for connections to the Site. However, no transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure.

Information saved in your browser, including student login credentials, is only as secure as the device and browser used. Other people with access to the same device or browser may be able to access it. Do not reuse passwords from other accounts, and avoid entering confidential or sensitive personal information into browser-based tools or student profiles.

11. Your Rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Request data portability

These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions. To make a request, contact us using the details in section 14.

12. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal information, you may raise a complaint by emailing jon.ellisdon@gridlock-holmes.com with the subject line "Data Protection Complaint". Please include details of your concern and the action you would like us to take.

We will acknowledge your complaint within 7 days and aim to provide a full response within 28 days. If the matter is complex and we need more time, we will let you know.

If you remain dissatisfied, you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's independent data protection authority. Its helpline is 0303 123 1113.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post any revised version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
