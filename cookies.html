Cookie Policy

Last updated: April 15, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device when you visit them. They are widely used to make websites work, improve functionality, and provide information to site owners. Similar technologies include localStorage, sessionStorage, and tracking pixels.

This policy covers cookies and all similar technologies used by Gridlock Holmes at gridlock-holmes.com ("the Site").

3. Your Consent

When you first visit the Site, you will see a cookie consent banner that allows you to:

  • Accept all cookies — enables analytics and advertising cookies
  • Reject non-essential cookies — only strictly necessary technologies will be used
  • Manage preferences — choose which categories of cookies to allow

Non-essential cookies are not set until you give consent. Your consent preference is stored in localStorage and lasts for 6 months, after which you will be asked again.

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Preferences" link in the Site footer.

4. How to Delete Cookies

You can delete cookies and clear localStorage at any time through your browser settings. Here is how to do it in common browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Clear browsing data
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data > Clear Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings > Privacy, search and services > Clear browsing data

Please note that deleting cookies and localStorage data will remove your learning progress and any saved grid layouts. This data cannot be recovered.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies. The third parties that may set cookies through the Site include:

  • Google (Analytics and AdSense) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Netlify — Netlify Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare — Cloudflare Privacy Policy (may be used for CDN and security)

We encourage you to review the privacy and cookie policies of these third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

Gridlock Holmes is designed to be used in educational settings, including by young people. We do not set non-essential cookies for users who have not consented. The localStorage data used for learning progress does not contain personal information beyond an optional username chosen by the student, and this data is never transmitted to our servers. Teachers and parents should be aware that Google Analytics and AdSense cookies, if consented to, may collect data as described in section 2.

7. Your Rights Under UK GDPR and PECR

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), you have the right to:

  • Be informed about what cookies we use and why
  • Give or refuse consent for non-essential cookies
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Request access to any personal data collected via cookies
  • Request deletion of any personal data collected via cookies

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at jon.ellisdon@gridlock-holmes.com. For more information about your data protection rights, visit the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

If you wish to make a complaint about our use of cookies or handling of your data, please follow our Data Protection Complaints Procedure. If you remain unsatisfied, you can escalate to the ICO.
