Support

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use Gridlock Holmes?
Click the "? How to Use" button at the top of the generator, or check out our CSS Tutorial for detailed tutorials.
Is Gridlock Holmes really free?
Yes! The core grid generator is 100% free forever. No signup, no credit card, no hidden fees. We offer Pro features for advanced users, but the essential tools are always free.
Can I use the generated code in commercial projects?
Absolutely! The code you generate is yours to use however you like — personal projects, commercial work, client sites, anything.
What's the difference between Grid and Flexbox?
Great question! Check out our Grid vs Flexbox guide which explains when to use each layout system.
My grid isn't working. What should I check?
Common issues:
  • Spaces vs commas: Use spaces to separate values (e.g., 1fr 1fr not 1fr, 1fr)
  • Missing units: Use 200px not just 200
  • Browser support: CSS Grid works in all modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
  • Use our Grid Detective tool to check for errors!
How do I save my layouts?
Use the Save section in the generator. Enter a name and click "Save". Your layouts are stored in your browser's localStorage and will persist between sessions.
What are Pro features?
Pro includes advanced features like:
  • Auto-responsive columns (auto-fit/minmax)
  • Named grid areas for complex layouts
  • 30+ professional template examples
  • Component library (React/Vue/Svelte)
  • Export to JSON and CodePen
  • No advertisements
  • Priority email support
Click "Get Pro" to see pricing options!
Does Gridlock Holmes work on mobile?
Yes! The site is fully responsive. However, for the best experience creating grids, we recommend using a desktop or tablet with a keyboard.
Can you add support for [feature]?
Maybe! Send us a feature request. We prioritize features based on user feedback and technical feasibility.
Who created Gridlock Holmes?
Gridlock Holmes was created to make CSS Grid accessible to developers of all skill levels. Learn more on our About page.
Data Protection

Data Protection & Privacy Complaints

We take your data protection rights seriously. If you have a concern about how we handle your personal data, cookies, or privacy, you can raise a complaint using the process below.

How to raise a data protection complaint

  • 1
    Email your complaint
    Send details of your concern to jon.ellisdon@gridlock-holmes.com with the subject line "Data Protection Complaint". Include your name, what data you believe is affected, and what action you would like us to take.
  • 2
    We acknowledge within 7 days
    We will confirm receipt of your complaint and let you know who is handling it.
  • 3
    Full response within 28 days
    We will investigate your complaint and respond with a full explanation of our findings, any action we have taken, and your options if you remain unsatisfied. If the matter is complex, we may extend this to a maximum of three months, but we will inform you within the first 28 days if this is necessary.
Your rights include: access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion of your data, objection to processing, withdrawal of consent, and data portability. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

Formal data subject requests (such as access, deletion, or correction requests under UK GDPR) will be responded to within one calendar month, as required by law.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's independent data protection authority:

Information Commissioner's Office
Website: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint
Helpline: 0303 123 1113
